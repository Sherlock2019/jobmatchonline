Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

JobsMatchNow ("we", "us") operates the JobsMatchNow app and website (jobsmatchnow.com), a mutual-matching platform connecting job candidates and recruiters. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number (optional), and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text).
  • Profile information: job title, skills, languages, years of experience, education, certifications, work history, location, salary expectations, availability, and — if you choose to add them — birthdate, nationality, work authorization, and links to your portfolio/GitHub/publications.
  • Uploaded content: your profile photo (including photos taken directly in the app), resume/CV files, and — for recruiters — company logos.
  • Usage data: the matches, likes/passes, messages, scheduled calls, meeting invitations, and private notes you create while using the app.
  • Recommendations: testimonials from people you invite to vouch for you, including the recommender's email address if you provide one.

How we use your information

We use this information to operate the core matching service: computing job/candidate fit, showing your profile to the other side once you've mutually matched, enabling in-app messaging and meeting scheduling, and sending emails you explicitly trigger (such as inviting someone to leave you a recommendation, or a calendar invite for a scheduled call).

How we store your information

Your data is stored in a private database that we operate and control. Uploaded files (photos, resumes) are stored on our servers and, where configured, mirrored to encrypted cloud storage for durability. All traffic between your device and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

Third parties

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not run advertising or third-party analytics/tracking scripts on the site. The only outside services involved are:

  • Email delivery: transactional emails you trigger (e.g. a recommendation invite) are sent through our email delivery provider.
  • Calendar links: "Add to calendar" buttons open Google Calendar or Outlook in your own browser or app, using a link that already contains the event details — we don't share your data with those services beyond what's in the link you choose to open.

Your choices

You can edit or remove most profile information at any time from within the app, delete private notes you've written, and cancel a pending recommendation request before it's used. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at the email below.

Children's privacy

JobsMatchNow is a job-matching platform intended for working-age adults and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "last updated" date above.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to privacy@jobsmatchnow.com.
