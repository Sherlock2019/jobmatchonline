3. Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we may collect: name, email address, account type, password or authentication credentials, language, country, email-verification status, and account creation/login information. Passwords are stored using secure password-hashing methods — we do not store passwords in readable plain text.

Candidate information. Candidates may provide: profile photo, phone number, general location, résumé/CV, employment history, education, skills and qualifications, languages, preferred roles, salary expectations, work-location preferences, remote/hybrid preferences, availability and notice period, work-authorization information, and job interests or deal-breakers. Unless clearly marked as required, these fields are optional and controlled by the candidate.

Recruiter and employer information. Recruiters and employers may provide: professional name and contact details, company name, business email address, job title, company website, company address and country, company-registration information, employer-verification documents, job descriptions, salary ranges, and work-location and scheduling information.

Matching and recruitment activity. We may process: jobs viewed, candidate and recruiter interest, mutual matches, applications, messages, interview invitations, interview status, feedback, offers, hiring outcomes, profile and job preferences, reasons supplied for accepting or declining an opportunity, and recruiter activity/response information.

Communications. We collect communications sent through JobsMatchNow, including in-app messages, support requests, reports and complaints, candidate feedback, moderation appeals, and email-notification status. Private messages are not intended to be routinely read by administrators. Authorized personnel may access relevant messages when necessary to investigate a report, prevent fraud, resolve a support issue, comply with law, or protect users.

Payment and subscription information. For paid recruiter subscriptions, we may receive: subscription plan, subscription status, billing period, transaction identifier, payment amount and currency, payment status, refund or chargeback status, and limited billing-contact information. Payments may be processed by Apple, Google Play, VNPay, Stripe, PayPal, or another payment provider shown at checkout. JobsMatchNow does not store complete payment-card numbers, card security codes, or raw Apple Pay/Google Pay credentials — payment providers process that information under their own privacy policies.

Device, technical, and usage information. We may automatically collect: IP address, device and browser type, operating system, app version, login timestamps, security events, crash and error information, pages and features used, referral source, cookie or session identifiers, and approximate country or region. We do not use this information to sell targeted advertising.

Information collected with device permission. When a feature requires device access, the application may request permission for camera, photo library, files or documents, notifications, or approximate/precise location. We request access only when needed for a feature, such as uploading a profile photo, attaching a résumé, or receiving job notifications. You can change device permissions through your operating-system settings at any time.

4. How we use personal information

We use personal information to: create and secure accounts; build candidate and recruiter profiles; display relevant employment opportunities; generate and explain match recommendations; confirm mutual interest; enable messaging; schedule interviews; manage applications and recruitment stages; verify recruiters and employers; process recruiter subscriptions; provide customer support; deliver service and security notifications; prevent fake jobs, fraud, abuse, and spam; enforce our Code of Good Conduct; investigate reports and appeals; improve application performance and usability; and comply with legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

5. Matching and automated assistance

JobsMatchNow may use automated systems to help: structure job requirements; compare candidate preferences with job conditions; identify skills that appear relevant; explain why a job or candidate may be suitable; detect spam, suspicious activity, or inappropriate language; and draft recruitment feedback for human review.

Match scores are recommendations, not guarantees. Automated systems do not independently make a final employment decision on behalf of an employer — recruiters and candidates remain responsible for their own decisions.

We do not intentionally use physical appearance, ethnicity, religion, pregnancy, sexual orientation, health status, or other protected characteristics to calculate job suitability.

6. When information is shared

Between candidates and recruiters. When a candidate and recruiter interact, we may share information necessary for the recruitment process. Depending on the user's privacy settings and recruitment stage, this may include name, profile information, skills, work experience, résumé, salary preferences, availability, messages, and interview information. We do not make private recruiter feedback visible to unrelated employers.

With service providers. We may use service providers for cloud hosting, database infrastructure, authentication, email delivery, push notifications, file storage, product analytics, error monitoring, payment processing, and security/fraud prevention. Providers receive only the information reasonably necessary to perform their services and must handle it under appropriate contractual or legal safeguards.

With authorities or professional advisers. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to a lawful request, protect users or the public, investigate fraud or serious misconduct, establish or defend legal claims, or complete a financing, restructuring, merger, or acquisition. Any prospective buyer would receive only information necessary for due diligence and remain subject to confidentiality obligations.

7. International data transfers

JobsMatchNow may use cloud hosting, analytics, communications, authentication, payment, and support providers located in different countries.

As a result, personal information may be processed outside the country where the user lives.

Where appropriate, JobsMatchNow will use contractual, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information during international processing.

9. Account deletion

Users can request account deletion through Settings → Account → Delete account, or by emailing privacy@jobsmatchnow.com.

When an account is deleted, we delete or anonymize associated personal information unless retention is necessary for legal obligations, accounting records, fraud prevention, safety investigations, active disputes, or enforcement records.

Deleting an account does not automatically cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription — you may also need to cancel through your Apple or Google subscription settings.

10. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, users may request to: access their personal information; correct inaccurate information; update profile information; download certain information; withdraw consent where processing relies on consent; restrict certain processing; object to certain uses; delete their account; report unauthorized use; and appeal significant moderation decisions.

Send requests to privacy@jobsmatchnow.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Candidates control relevant visibility settings. Where supported, candidates may: hide their profile; limit profile visibility; remove their photo; replace or delete their résumé; disable job notifications; block recruiters; report employers; and withdraw from a recruitment process.

Candidates should not upload confidential documents or information they are not authorized to share.

JobsMatchNow uses necessary cookies and similar technologies for authentication, security, session management, language preferences, application functionality, and basic performance measurement. Where legally required, non-essential analytics or marketing technologies will not be activated without appropriate consent.

JobsMatchNow does not currently display third-party advertising.

13. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, which may include encryption in transit, password hashing, access controls, administrator authentication, audit logging, secure hosting, backup procedures, security monitoring, and restricted access to candidate information.

No system is completely secure. Users should use strong passwords, protect their devices, and notify us immediately about suspected unauthorized access.

14. Recruitment safety

Recruiters and employers must not request improper payments from candidates. Candidates should report: fake vacancies; requests for application fees; requests for cryptocurrency; requests for gift cards; suspicious equipment deposits; identity theft; harassment; discrimination; or misuse of personal information.

Reports can be submitted through the application or by emailing support@jobsmatchnow.com.
